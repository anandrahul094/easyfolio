# Compliance checklist — not legal advice

- Describe the service as factual mutual-fund discovery/research.
- Avoid personalized recommendations, suitability claims, buy/sell calls or return promises in the MVP.
- Do not process transactions or SIP instructions.
- Have Indian securities counsel assess the complete user journey, outbound links, integrations and monetization against applicable SEBI IA, RA, EOP and other requirements.
- Display source and NAV date, market-risk disclosures, terms, privacy policy and a grievance/corrections contact.
- Verify each external destination before publishing it.
